About IAN-XAU-AI

IAN-XAU-AI is a trading software concept focused on XAUUSD (gold) market analysis and strategy-based trade execution.

How IAN-XAU-AI Works

The system is designed to analyze market structure, including Asia High, Asia Low, previous day high (PDH), and previous day low (PDL), before considering a trade.

Frequently Asked Questions

What market does IAN-XAU-AI focus on?

IAN-XAU-AI focuses on XAUUSD, commonly known as gold against the US dollar.

Does IAN-XAU-AI guarantee profits?

No. Trading involves risk, and no trading system can guarantee profits.

What is the Asia High and Asia Low?

They are the highest and lowest prices reached during the defined Asian trading session and can be used as reference levels.

What are PDH and PDL?

PDH means Previous Day High and PDL means Previous Day Low. They are commonly used reference levels in technical analysis.
