THE SYSTEM

What does it watch?

The strategy focuses on specific price levels and confirmation instead of entering randomly.

01

Asia High

The high created during the Asia session can become an important liquidity reference.

02

Asia Low

The Asia session low is monitored as a possible liquidity and reaction area.

03

PDH / PDL

Previous Day High and Previous Day Low are used as important reference levels.

04

Confirmation

A level alone is not enough. The strategy looks for price reaction before an entry.

STRATEGY

Simple rules. Defined conditions.

The system follows a structured process instead of chasing every market movement.

01 — Mark the levels

  • Asia High
  • Asia Low
  • Previous Day High
  • Previous Day Low

02 — Watch liquidity

  • Price approaches a key level
  • Liquidity may be taken
  • Market reaction is monitored

03 — Wait for confirmation

  • Price reaction
  • Market structure confirmation
  • Entry condition must be satisfied

04 — Define the risk

  • Stop loss defined before entry
  • Take profit defined
  • No guaranteed outcome
