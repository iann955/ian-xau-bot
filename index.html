Strategy Reference

  • Mark Asia High and Asia Low using New York time.
  • Mark PDH and PDL from the previous New York calendar day.
  • Look for liquidity sweeps and a clear reaction.
  • Wait for confirmation before considering an entry.
  • Define stop-loss and take-profit before any trade.

Session Definition

  • Asia range: 7:00 PM to midnight, New York time.
  • Before 7:00 PM New York time, the latest completed Asia range is used.
  • After 7:00 PM, the current Asia range is still forming until midnight.
  • PDH/PDL: previous New York calendar day, midnight to midnight.
