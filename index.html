TRADING STRATEGY

01 · Identify Liquidity

Mark Asia High, Asia Low, Previous Day High and Previous Day Low. These levels can attract price and liquidity.

02 · Wait for a Sweep

Watch whether price moves beyond a key level and then returns. A level touch alone is not a trade signal.

03 · Confirm the Reaction

Look for a clear reaction and confirmation on the selected timeframe before considering an entry.

04 · Manage Risk

Define entry, stop loss and take profit before entering. Avoid trades where the risk is not justified by the setup.
